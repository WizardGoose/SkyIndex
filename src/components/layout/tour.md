`bold+red+blue:Jokes aside` - Skydex is a completely `green:free `and [`blue:open-source`](https://github.com/WizardGoose/Skydex) utility, entirely designed for ease of understanding!

- [Profile](/island) - Displays all of the available stats/information from the API
(if you use the [Skydex Mod](https://github.com/WizardGoose/Skydex) you will be able to access things like your sacks and island chests! The mod only sends data to your locally hosted Skydex, never anywhere else.)
- [Recipes](/recipes) - Display of all of the possible recipes in Hypixel SkyBlock! Can redirect you to the [Forge](/forge), or the [Greenhouse](/greenhouse)!
- [Forge](/forge) - Display of all of the recipes available in the forge!
- [Greenhouse](/greenhouse) - Breakdown of items (i.e all required mutations, plantings, and growth cycles for a rose dragon!), including a solver and a designer!!
- [Shards](/shards) - Fusion paths, recipes and rates!
